Frequently asked questions

What does FSHM help with?

Scholarship discovery, research-programme guidance, supervisor outreach, application strategy and document support for master's and PhD study.

Does FSHM guarantee admission or a scholarship?

No. Universities and funding bodies make the final admission and funding decisions. FSHM helps you prepare a stronger, better-targeted application.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

It applies to FSHM Complete and is subject to written eligibility and refund terms. Read them before you enrol.

Who can use FSHM?

International students from any country, including those outside Africa, applying to master's or PhD programmes.

What documents should I prepare?

Academic CV, transcripts, a draft statement of intent, research interests and referee details. We help you refine them.

Can I book a one-to-one consultation?

Yes. Use any Book Consultation button to choose a time on Calendly.
